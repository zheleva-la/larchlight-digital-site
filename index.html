Hello, I'm Laura

I work in healthcare, and I'm passionate about making digital accessible to everyone, whether you're confident with computers or not.

It all started with helping my husband and family with their websites. I soon realised how much I enjoy it, and it's now what I love to do in my spare time. A clear, friendly website makes a real difference to a small business, and I'd love to help you too.

Away from the screen, you'll find me with my family, my daughter and our dog, usually outdoors and ideally somewhere with a good sunset. That's where the name comes from: Larchlight blends the golden larches of the New Forest with a little bit of me.

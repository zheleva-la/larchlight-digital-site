How it works

Three simple steps, and you're never left wondering what happens next.

  1. 1

    We have a chat

    Tell me about your business and what you need, over a cuppa or a call. No forms to wrestle with.

  2. 2

    I design and build it

    I use Claude, an AI assistant, to speed up the design and HTML coding, then check and test every page myself on a phone, tablet and computer. You see it take shape and tell me what you think.

  3. 3

    You go live, with support

    I get it online and stay on hand for changes, so it keeps working for you.
